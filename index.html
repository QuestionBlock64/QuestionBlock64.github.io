／ About Me ＼

The dev behind the block

Indie game developer and software modder with a passion for reverse engineering and game physics.

I spend a lot of time analyzing momentum and character movement kits from classic franchises like Super Mario and Sonic the Hedgehog. That deep dive into game feel directly inspires my own projects.

Currently, I'm engineering a custom standalone 2D platformer in the Godot engine — a personal project dedicated to a close friend who stars as the protagonist. On the modding front, I'm leading development of the MiSide Multiplayer Mod, pioneering multiplayer network synchronization and animation handling with the BepInEx framework.

Beyond code, I'm often lost in complex video game lore or experimenting with Windows OS architecture and kernel-level behaviors. Every day is a new puzzle to solve.
